🔭 Astronomy
Sky Maps
Each map is its own circular sky chart — tag it by hemisphere and season. Add existing celestial objects to it and set their position for this specific chart.
Celestial Objects
The shared registry of every star, planet, moon, and asteroid in your world. Each one can be placed on any number of sky maps, at a different position on each — go into a map to place an object on it.
Coordinate Systems
Axis 1 wraps around the outside of the circle, like a compass. Axis 2 measures distance from center — the higher end of its range plots at the center, the lower end at the outer edge.
